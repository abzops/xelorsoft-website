XS Arena · Gaming centre and snooker parlour software

Game centre software for snooker, PS5 and RC tracks

XS Arena shows every snooker and pool table, PS5 console and RC track in your centre on one live floor. Start a session with one tap, let the timer count the money, add snacks, and close the bill by cash or UPI, without mental maths on a busy Saturday night.

Ask for a demo →XS Arena opens to its first centres in November 2026.

One floor for every activity

Most tools handle only snooker or only gaming consoles. XS Arena is built for mixed centres, so snooker and pool tables, PS5 consoles, RC drift and offroad tracks sit side by side on one screen, each with its own rates.

Features

Live floor

Every table, console and track as a card: free, running, paused or time up, with the live running cost.

Session timers

Start, pause, extend or move a session to another table. The timer keeps counting even if the internet drops.

Walk-ins and waiting list

Seat walk-ins in seconds and keep a waiting list when the floor is full.

Billing

Time charges, snacks and drinks on one bill, paid by cash, UPI or both.

Expenses and profit

Record rent, salaries and stock so you see what the centre really earns.

Staff logins

Owner, manager and front desk each get their own login and permissions.

Shift close

Count the cash at the end of a shift and see any difference straight away.

Who XS Arena is for

Snooker and pool parlours, PS5 and gaming lounges, RC car tracks and multi-activity fun centres.

Questions

Which activities does XS Arena support?

Snooker and pool tables, PS5 and other consoles, RC drift and offroad tracks, and custom activities with their own rates.

What happens if the internet drops during a game?

Timers keep running on the device and the session syncs when the connection comes back, so no game time is lost.

Can customers pay by UPI?

Yes. A bill can be paid by cash, UPI or a mix of both.

Does it work on a tablet at the counter?

Yes. XS Arena runs in the browser on tablets, phones and laptops.

How do I get XS Arena for my centre?

Email support@xelorsoft.com. We set up your floor, rates and staff with you.
