Questions

What people ask before a first call.

Send us your question

Do I need full specs to start?

No. Tell us your goal, who it is for and what is hard today.

Can we start with a small website or app?

Yes. One workflow, a landing page or a small app is a good start.

How is the price of a project set?

It depends on the scope and the integrations. We agree the price with you before work starts.

How long do software projects take?

We set the dates together. Review rounds, content and the services you connect to all shape the plan.

Can XS Connect reply in Malayalam?

Yes. It asks each customer for English or Malayalam and replies in that language.

What support do you give after launch?

Hosting, access and fixes are written into the project agreement.

How a project runs, in three steps

  1. Agree the scope and the price

    We talk to the people who will use it, write down the first version and fix the price before we build.

  2. Review the software as we build it

    You see a working version early and comment on it while we build, on your phone or laptop.

  3. Launch, hand over and support

    We set up hosting, email and access in your business’s name, and agree the support you need after launch.
