A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Before we
get started.

Is my project a good fit?＋

We work with startups and small businesses on custom software, websites and AI integrations. Tell us who the result is for and what it should help them do. That gives us a practical starting point to discuss fit.

Can we start with a smaller first version?＋

Yes. A focused MVP or a single workflow can be a useful starting point. We can discuss the essential tasks first, then shape a scope that fits your priorities.

How much will it cost, and how long will it take?＋

Scope, integrations and complexity determine the budget and timeline. Share your goals, current tools and target timing so we can discuss an appropriate plan. Pricing and delivery dates are agreed for each project.

Can AI fit into the tools we already use?＋

That depends on your tools, available integrations and data. We start by understanding the workflow, then discuss where AI could help and where people should stay involved.
