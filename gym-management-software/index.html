Why gym owners switch from the register

Most gyms in Kerala still track members in a notebook or a spreadsheet. Fees are missed, expired members keep training, and the owner only knows the day's collection after closing. XS Gym fixes the three things that cost a gym money every month:

  • Renewals that slip through. XS Gym lists who expires this week and who already owes you, so the front desk follows up before the money is lost.
  • Cash nobody can account for. Every payment and expense is recorded with who entered it and when, and the record can't be changed later without a trace.
  • Owners who can't step away. Today's collection, active members and dues are on your phone, wherever you are.

How it works

  1. Create your gym in XS Gym and add your membership plans.
  2. Add your members (or have the front desk add them as they come in).
  3. Record payments as you collect them. XS Gym handles reminders, dues and reports from there.

Who XS Gym is for

XS Gym is made for independent gyms, ladies' gyms, fitness studios, CrossFit and functional training boxes, martial arts and boxing academies, and yoga studios across Kerala, from Kochi and Thrissur to Thiruvananthapuram and Kozhikode.

Questions

Does XS Gym work on a mobile phone?

Yes. XS Gym runs in the browser on phones, tablets and laptops, so the owner and the front desk can use it on the devices they already have. There is nothing to install.

Can it send fee reminders on WhatsApp?

Yes. XS Gym prepares the reminder for each member who is due or expiring, in English or Malayalam, and opens it in WhatsApp so your staff can send it with one tap.

Does it understand Malayalam?

Yes. Reminders can be sent in Malayalam, and the XS AI voice assistant understands spoken English and Malayalam, including Malayalam names.

Can my staff use it without seeing everything?

Yes. Each staff member gets their own login as Reception, Trainer or Manager, and sees only what that role allows. You can switch a login off at any time.

Can I pause a member's membership?

Yes. You can pause a membership for 7 to 180 days and the end date moves forward automatically. Every change is recorded.

Where is my gym's data stored?

On secure servers in Mumbai, India. Each gym's data is kept separate, and every change to payments and memberships is recorded with who made it.
