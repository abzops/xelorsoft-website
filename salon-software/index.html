Questions

How much does XS Salon cost?

The first 14 days are free. We share the monthly plan with you before your trial ends, so you decide with real use behind you.

My salon is not GST registered. Can I still use it?

Yes. GST is off by default. Turn it on in settings only if your salon is registered.

Do I need to install anything?

No. XS Salon runs in the browser on phones, tablets and laptops. You can add it to your home screen like an app.

How do clients book online?

Your salon gets its own booking link. Share it on Instagram, WhatsApp or your Google listing; clients choose the service, stylist and time, and the booking appears on your calendar.

Can my staff have their own logins?

Yes. Each team member gets a login, and you choose what they can see and do.

Does it send WhatsApp messages?

XS Salon prepares booking and bill messages in English or Malayalam and opens them in WhatsApp. Your staff press send.
