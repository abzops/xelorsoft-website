Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information Xelorsoft (“we”, “us”) collects, why we collect it and the choices you have. It covers this website, our client work and our products, XS Connect and XS Gym.

1. This website

xelorsoft.com does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits in aggregate without cookies or personal profiles. Our hosting and network providers (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) process technical data such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

2. When you contact us

If you email us or use the enquiry form (which opens your own email app), we receive the details you choose to share: usually your name, contact details, business name and message. We use them to reply, discuss your project and keep a record of our conversation. We do not sell this information or use it for unrelated marketing.

3. Client projects

When we build software, websites or content for a client, we handle any personal data in that project on the client’s instructions and only for the agreed work. Access, retention and handover arrangements are set out in the project agreement.

4. XS Connect and XS Gym

Our products process information on behalf of the businesses that use them. For XS Connect, this includes messages, voice notes and contact details exchanged with a business through WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook once that business connects its accounts. For XS Gym, it includes member and operational records the gym adds.

  • We use this information only to provide, secure and support the service for that business.
  • Information received through Meta platforms (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook) is used only to deliver the features the business has turned on. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with data brokers.
  • AI features send the minimum content needed to our AI and speech providers (for example Google Gemini and Sarvam AI) to generate replies or transcripts. They act as our processors.
  • Product data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in its Mumbai (India) region.

5. Service providers

We rely on carefully chosen providers for hosting (Cloudflare, GitHub, Supabase), email (Zoho Mail), messaging (Meta) and AI processing (Google, Sarvam AI). Each receives only what it needs to perform its service.

6. How long we keep information

We keep enquiries and project correspondence for as long as needed to respond, deliver the work and meet legal obligations. Product data is kept while the business uses the service and deleted or returned when the account is closed, unless the law requires otherwise.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls and reputable infrastructure providers. No system is perfectly secure, so we limit the data we collect and who can access it.

8. Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information. If you messaged a business that uses XS Connect, you can ask that business, or us, to delete your conversation data. Email privacy@xelorsoft.com with the phone number or account the request relates to. We will confirm your request and act on it within 30 days.

We follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and apply the same care to visitors from other countries.

9. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the effective date above.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@xelorsoft.com. General enquiries: info@xelorsoft.com.
