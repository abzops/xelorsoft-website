# Scroll brief

Self-authored under creative delegation: the owner asked for a scroll-based rebuild using scroll-craft, with the content unchanged. Nothing below adds or alters copy.

## Constraints carried over
- Content frozen (words, links, nine-section order), enforced by `scripts/check-content.mjs`.
- Manrope, approved logo, Carbon #171719 and Porcelain #F6F6F4.
- No invented clients, metrics or testimonials, so no counters.
- No generated imagery or decorative hero objects; the brand's asset is its sentences.

## Grammar
Typographic poster. The other grammars need photography or footage (filmic, continuous world, gallery), a two-sided argument (split stage), real-time data (live surface), long-form chapters (chaptered editorial) or many short cuts (cutlist). This page is short sentences at different weights, which is what the poster grammar is built for.

Tell-someone sentence: "It's the site where one full stop follows you down the page and lands on every heading."

## Signature move
The travelling full stop. The wordmark ends on a period and so does almost every heading. A single dot, measured from the Manrope glyph itself, sits exactly on the period of the heading in view; when the next heading arrives it lifts off, stretches along an arc and drops into place. Its last landing is the point of the closing question mark. It is presentation only (aria-hidden) and disabled under reduced motion.
