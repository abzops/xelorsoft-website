XS Connect · WhatsApp CRM and AI inbox

A WhatsApp CRM that answers your leads in Malayalam

XS Connect brings WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook messages into one inbox. Its AI asks each customer whether they prefer English or Malayalam, answers questions about your business, captures the lead and hands the big orders to your team.

Open XS Connect →First 100 AI conversations free, then ₹5 per conversation.

Every lead in one inbox

Small businesses lose leads because messages are spread across WhatsApp, Instagram DMs and the Facebook page, and nobody replies at night. XS Connect gathers them in one list, replies within seconds, and shows your team which chats need a person.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your business, or fill in your Brand Brain.
  2. We connect your WhatsApp Business number, Instagram and Facebook Page with you.
  3. XS Connect starts answering; your team takes over whenever it's needed.

Who XS Connect is for

Shops, boutiques, restaurants and bakeries, clinics, coaching centres, travel agencies, gyms and salons in Kerala that get more messages than they can answer on time.

Questions

Can XS Connect reply in Malayalam?

Yes. It asks each customer whether they prefer English or Malayalam, saves that choice, and replies in that language from then on.

How much does XS Connect cost?

The first 100 AI conversations are free. After that it costs ₹5 per conversation.

Will the AI make promises I can't keep?

XS Connect answers from the information in your Brand Brain. Refunds and unclear questions are handed to your staff, and you can switch on approval mode so a person checks each reply.

Does it work with Instagram and Facebook too?

Yes. WhatsApp Business, Instagram and Facebook Page messages come into the same inbox.

Can it understand voice notes?

Yes. Voice notes are transcribed, including Malayalam, so they can be answered like text messages.

Will it message customers after 24 hours?

No. XS Connect follows WhatsApp's rules and does not send free-form messages outside the 24-hour customer service window.
