# Design direction

Nov 2026: the owners asked to remove Brand identity, Social & content and AI-assisted video, and to rebuild with taste-skill so the site does not look AI-made. The scroll-craft build is in Git history.

## Rules

- Page kind: software (a SaaS company that also builds for clients). No location or "studio" wording. The taste-skill deal: hero collage, mega nav, footer columns, closing type, one dark band, tonal buttons, sky ground, pinned-chapters on Process.
- Brand: Carbon #171719, Porcelain #F6F6F4, Manrope (self-hosted). Use the approved logo only.
- Show the product, not decoration: every section carries real, readable UI (XS Connect inbox and chat, XS Gym members, AI notes, checklists, voice note, channel hub).
- Plain, specific copy. Every heading names a thing. No invented clients, metrics or testimonials. Sample names in the UI are illustrative.
- Native scrolling; reduced motion shows still pictures. Without JavaScript all content is visible.

## Sections

1. Hero: "New" notice for the newest products, "Software for gyms, salons, game centres and sales teams", XS Connect inbox with an app switcher card listing all four apps.
2. Our products (#products): four equal cards (newest first, "New" badge): XS Salon, XS Arena, XS Gym, XS Connect, each linking to its product page and app. When a product stops being new, drop its badge and move it after the newer ones.
3. XS Connect: inbox and a Malayalam chat.
4. Services (four): custom software (XS Gym), AI tools, websites, Malayalam voice notes. The voice card is hidden on phones so the section fits two screens.
5. Channels (dark band): WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook.
6. Questions (FAQ).
7. Process, three pinned steps.
8. Contact: email links, social links and a form that composes an email to info@.
9. Closing line and footer.

Contact destinations: info@xelorsoft.com (enquiries), support@xelorsoft.com (product support), privacy@xelorsoft.com (privacy), Instagram /xelorsoft, LinkedIn /company/xelorsoft, Facebook /xelorsoft. No phone number yet.
