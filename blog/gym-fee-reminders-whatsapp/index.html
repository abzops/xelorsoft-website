How to send gym fee reminders on WhatsApp

By Abhinand · 21 November 2026 · 4 min read

Most members who miss a fee have not decided to leave. They lost track of the date. A short WhatsApp message a few days before the membership ends brings most of them to the desk on time, and it costs nothing.

When to send a reminder

Three messages are enough for one renewal:

  • Three days before the end date. A friendly note with the plan, the end date and the renewal amount.
  • On the end date. One line: today is the last day, and the desk can renew it in a minute.
  • Three days after, if they still have not paid. Keep it polite and ask whether they are coming back. If there is no reply, call them.

More than that and members mute your chat, which means your next message is lost too. Send between 8 in the morning and 8 at night.

What to write

Use the member's name, the date and the amount. A message with all three looks like it came from the gym, and it answers the question before they ask it.

Before the end date

Hi Arjun, your 3-month membership at Fit Zone ends on Friday, 28 November. Renewal for the next 3 months is 4,500. You can pay at the desk or reply here and we'll send a payment link.

On the end date

Hi Arjun, today is the last day of your Fit Zone membership. Renew at the desk this evening and your training continues without a break.

After the end date

Hi Arjun, we haven't seen you since your membership ended on 28 November. Are you planning to continue? If something changed, tell us and we can pause it instead.

Write them in the language you usually use with the member. A reminder in the language they reply in gets answered faster.

Three ways to send them

1. By hand from the gym's phone

Install the free WhatsApp Business app on the gym's phone. Save each reminder as a quick reply, then type / in a chat to insert it and change the name, date and amount.

This works for a small gym. The hard part is the list: someone has to go through the register every morning to find who expires in three days.

2. Broadcast lists

A broadcast list in WhatsApp Business sends one message to many contacts at once, and each member sees it as a private message. Two limits matter for fee reminders:

  • It only reaches members who have saved the gym's number in their phone.
  • Everyone gets the same text, so you cannot put each member's own date and amount in it.

Broadcasts are better for general news: a holiday closure, new timings, a new batch.

3. Software that builds the list

Gym software keeps every member's end date and balance, so it can show you each morning who is due, who ended yesterday and who still owes money. Keep a note of what happened

When a member replies "will pay on Monday", write it down next to their name. Otherwise two staff members remind the same person on the same day, and the member feels chased.

Three mistakes to avoid

  • Fee reminders in a group. Adding members to a WhatsApp group shows everyone's phone number to everyone else, and an unpaid member's name in a group embarrasses them. Keep reminders one to one.
  • A threatening tone. "Pay today or your membership will be cancelled" loses members who meant to pay. The third message asks whether they are coming back. That question brings in more renewals than a warning.
  • Late-night messages. A reminder at 11 at night is read as rude, whatever it says.
